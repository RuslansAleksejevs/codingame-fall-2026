# PPO self-play v1 protocol

Recorded before PPO training or opening new confirmation maps, 2026-11-06.

- Start from the exact BC v1 actor, SHA256 `b97859bc318a0bcbbf9d9b3080da695313fd716018d50f8155902d558291e24e`, with the same 128 features, 24,833 actor parameters, action space and greedy deployment decoder. Add a separate training-only critic.
- All computation is local on the M4 MacBook Air, 24 GB. Four native rollout workers initially; benchmark CPU/MPS optimization. No external paid compute, live submission or main-branch promotion.
- Training uses the same 158 training maps as BC. The 42 BC validation maps form the checkpoint-selection panel. New final maps: seeds **7001–7050**, checked by content against all previous and training/selection maps.
- Self-play opponent mixture by complete episode: 50% current stochastic policy against itself (learn from both seats); 25% frozen greedy BC; 25% a uniformly sampled recent frozen stochastic policy, initially BC. Learner seat is balanced. Retain up to four recent snapshots, refreshed every 100,000 controlled-player decisions.
- Sampling temperature 0.7, fixed throughout training; final tournament uses the original greedy decoder for both BC and PPO. Both players act from the same pre-state without observing the other's current plan.
- PPO-Clip on the **joint full-turn log probability**, the sum over sequential legal categorical choices. Shared turn advantage; no per-unit ratio clipping. Terminal win/loss/draw rewards +1/-1/0; no shaped reward. GAE gamma 0.995, lambda 0.95, true game termination at 200 turns; complete episodes avoid truncated-episode bootstrap ambiguity.
- Initial optimizer: actor Adam at 3e-5, critic Adam at 3e-4, clip 0.1, four optimization epochs, gradient norm cap 0.5. Entropy coefficient 0.002 uses mean conditional choice entropy per turn. Stop additional actor epochs when empirical full-turn KL exceeds 0.03. Record actual KL/clip fraction, value loss, explained variance and all stop events.
- Correctness/speed pilot first; preserve it separately. Main run target **1,000,000 controlled-player decisions**, with a **60-minute training wall-time cap**, checked between complete rollout/update iterations. Do not change rewards or tune on confirmation outcomes. Stop on invalid actions, nonfinite tensors or unexplained log-probability/parity errors.
- Evaluate greedy checkpoints against frozen BC on all 42 selection maps/both seats at approximately 100k, 250k, 500k, 750k and 1M decisions, plus the final checkpoint if the time cap stops early. Choose the trained checkpoint with highest selection score rate; ties select the earlier checkpoint. Save/report the final checkpoint even if it is not selected. The initial BC control is not eligible to masquerade as a trained PPO candidate.
- Final original-referee panel: BC and selected PPO each against **v6, Lacoz 7f190183, khoufou 372c1cd6**, 50 new maps, both seats: **600 games**. Add 100 direct PPO-vs-BC games on the same maps. Run serially under unchanged 1000/50 ms limits; freeze sources and weights before the panel.
- Report all wins/draws/losses, errors/timeouts separately, matched clean-pair deltas and map-clustered intervals. This is one training seed, not an algorithm-wide claim. Retain unsuccessful runs and compact representative rollouts, checkpoints, seeds, hashes, commands and complete final match records on `rl/research`.

References: [PPO paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.06347), [GAE paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.02438), [PPO-Clip equations and KL early stopping](https://spinningup.openai.com/en/latest/algorithms/ppo.html). Accessed 2026-11-06; implementation claims and measurements are recorded separately.
